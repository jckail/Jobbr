# Direction: Job search command center

Create one polished, responsive desktop workspace for an ambitious professional's active job search. Preserve the real Jobbr logo asset, core Overview/Jobs/Pipeline/Profile navigation, existing Inter sans typography and token palette; overhaul hierarchy and density. Main outcome: immediately know what opportunity to prioritize and which action to take.

Use a compact polished 232px sidebar with crisp icons, Overview active, Jobs, Pipeline, Profile, a primary Add job action, subtle workspace summary, and a user/profile footer. Top content bar has a labeled search field, keyboard hint and compact workspace controls. Main header is “Your next chapter starts here.” with muted “A clear plan for your next move.” and a compact sample-data indicator. No invented personalized name.

A balanced two-column dashboard: wide central content with narrower right rail. A distinctive restrained lavender planning card says “Make your next move count” with next-best-action copy and “Explore best matches”. Four quiet metrics show tracked jobs, strong matches, active applications and median compensation, with source-appropriate labels and no fake deltas. A rich Best matches table/list shows three realistic sample roles with company text identity, title, compensation, remote/location, skill chips, fit 94/87/82, and View role links. Use small score rings plus readable labels. Add a compact pipeline strip with Saved, Applied, Screen, Interview, Offer counts, accompanied by Open pipeline.

Right rail: “Your focus today” has actionable “Review a strong match”, “Update your target profile”, “Prepare for your next conversation”, clearly recommendations rather than scheduled real events. Career coach card says “Close the gaps that matter” with 2 concise skill-gap insights, explaining that fit scores reflect profile overlap and preferences; CTA “Improve profile”. A profile-readiness summary should be honest sample content and link to Profile. Avoid generic chatbot textbox, fake sent applications, hiring probabilities, “live” claims, invented notifications or brand-company logos.

A premium feel through typography, whitespace, meticulous alignment, subtly elevated cards, clean borders, small purple details and purposeful line icons. No huge marketing hero, no charts that dominate decisions, no random illustration or stock photo. Use only defined neutral/purple/status colors. Include semantic navigation, 44px touch targets, visible focus and text accompanying color. At mobile widths stack content, compact header, hide sidebar, show bottom navigation, avoid global horizontal overflow. Buttons/links should have meaningful prototype destinations where possible. Sample values are clearly labeled and not represented as the user's data.

Sidebar logo must visibly render the canonical public asset URL (not J text, initials or invented SVG). Reusable sidebar may be reused or replaced structurally for the redesign, but the real logo must remain. Use ONLY the fonts, colors, spacing, and component styles defined in the design system. Do not introduce any fonts, colors, or visual styles not in the design system.
